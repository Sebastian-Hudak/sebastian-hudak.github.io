2. Personal Data Processed

This website may involve the following limited categories of personal data:

  • Technical access data: IP address, request date and time, browser or user agent, requested URL, referrer, approximate location derived from network data, and similar server log information.

Please do not send special categories of personal data, confidential information, or proprietary information through the contact form unless it is necessary and you are authorised to do so.

5. Retention

Personal data is kept only for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected. The criteria used are:

  • Contact messages are kept for as long as needed to respond and manage the relationship or request.
  • Messages related to projects, collaboration, or legal matters may be kept longer where necessary for records or legal claims.
  • Technical server logs are retained according to the hosting provider's security and operations policies.
  • Aggregated statistics that no longer identify a visitor may be kept for longer.

If you contact me, your message may be processed by the email services used by you and by me. Personal data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising purposes.

Some providers may be located outside the EU/EEA. Where such transfers occur, they are handled by those providers under their own terms, safeguards, and privacy notices. External links are not controlled by this website.

  • Right to restriction of processing in specific cases.
